# Thuban · 右枢

Leave a project that people can understand and agents can resume: clear responsibilities, useful document owners, discoverable task routes, and a structure that stays proportional to the work.

## Choose the work

- **Full governance:** “右枢”, “右枢一下”, or an equivalent execution request. Establish the current project boundary, understand its structure, repair directories and important documents, evolve needed nodes, update consumers, and verify the result. A named subproject narrows the scope; inspect affected parent routes without silently adopting its siblings.
- **Review or discussion:** inspect and answer within the request. Mentions, quotations, proposed wording, and “只看不改” do not start an execution pass.
- **Task maintenance:** after ordinary work, follow the project's Maintenance contract and update only what the task affected. Enter a relevant reference when the change actually needs it.

For an execution request, state the scope and intended changes, then complete authorized local, recoverable governance. This includes useful document repairs and evidence-backed node creation, splitting, merging, or retirement. Reuse authorization across the task; ask only for a material expansion or a decision the user still owns. Respect effective project constraints, protected records, and narrower ownership. External actions, irreversible loss, access changes, and business goals require their corresponding authorization. Read-only requests stay read-only.

## Decide from the project

- Give a subject one clear owner or evidence set. Distinguish actual behavior, intended behavior, operating instructions, and history; reconcile conflicts against evidence.
- Mark each owner document (one a node's Routes table names as owning a truth) with a one-line header under its title: what it owns, and how to maintain it — current facts and rules only, overwrite instead of appending by date, move process and history verbatim to evidence when it grows bulky (a checkup, not a hard cap). Date time-sensitive facts with "last verified" and an evidence link. Evidence, logs, and task records need no header.
- Reuse useful files and established structure. Add a document when a recurring task lacks an adequate owner. Prefer routing to the owner over reproducing its content.
- Grow a child node when a sustained area has distinct decisions or local context and its own gateway reduces recovery cost. Merge or retire it when that benefit disappears. Judge the work, not directory depth or a document quota.
- Keep local facts local. Root records cross-project structure and consequences. Update goals and priorities only from the user's decisions or approved plan.
- Read by task and stop when evidence is sufficient. Reuse what is known; reread after relevant changes, lost context, or a new uncertainty.
- Write contracts as positive defaults that tell an agent what to do. Reserve prohibitions for real invariants (privacy, money or permission semantics, irreversible or external actions). Do not add disclaimers such as “does not prove” or “is not authority” unless a real confusion occurred, and remove them once it is resolved: every extra prohibition makes agents more hesitant.
- Maintain human comprehension as well as agent routing: expose the overall framework and important-file responsibilities in an appropriate existing entry. No changed truth means no write.

## Finish

Verify the effects of the actual changes: routes and references, preserved unique content, relevant operation/build checks, and recovery. For substantial structural work, test whether an independent agent can resume representative tasks; details are in Governance. Report the result, meaningful changes, verification, and unresolved coverage or decisions. Match the report to the work.
